colour a horse

Three horses, drawn in charcoal and left uncoloured. Pick one, fill it in however you like, give it a name and send it over. I go through them by hand and the ones I fall for go up for adoption on my GitHub profile and in the paddock, credited to you.

These are brush drawings, so the outlines have gaps in them and the fill tool will run straight out through one. The brush is the honest way round it — paint goes underneath the charcoal, so going over a line does no harm.
